# Baselines — Quorum vs CNNDetection and FatFormer

_Generated by `scripts/compare_baselines.py` on 31 Aug 2026. Raw numbers:
`docs/baselines.json`._

Two published detectors, downloaded at their released weights and run **here**,
on our images, against our reals. Not their papers' numbers copied across — those
are measured against their own real pools (LSUN/ImageNet) and do not transfer.

| detector | ref | training data | weights |
|---|---|---|---|
| CNNDetection | Wang et al., CVPR 2020 | ProGAN, 20 classes, blur+JPEG aug | `blur_jpg_prob0.5.pth` (282MB) |
| FatFormer | Liu et al., CVPR 2024 | ProGAN, 4 classes | released ckpt (1.97GB) + OpenAI ViT-L/14 |
| Quorum | this repo | SID_Set + So-Fake (train split) | `data/models/*.npz` (3.5KB) |

## Protocol

Every image is `normalise()`d (JPEG q95) and degraded by `quorum.degrade` with
the same per-image seeding as `docs/robustness.md`, then handed to all four
detector configurations. Identical pixels in; each detector then applies its own
published preprocessing (FatFormer: resize 256 + centre crop 224; CNNDetection:
centre crop 224, reported both at native resolution — their protocol — and after
a resize, because resampling destroys the high-frequency statistics it reads).

Five of the 14 settings, one per transform family, harshest of each. AUROC only:
accuracy at 0.5 is in the JSON but is not comparable across detectors, which sit
at different operating points.

600 COCO val2017 reals vs 600 WildFake DALL·E-advanced fakes. Neither our
training set nor theirs contains either.

FatFormer at 0.252 under blur and 0.302 under 4× downscale is *inverted* — it
calls blurred DALL·E images real more often than it calls COCO photographs real.
Its 0.394 spread across the grid is the whole robustness argument in one row.

## Reading

1. **On the set that decides the competition we win by 0.33 AUROC**, and by 0.45
   averaged over the transform grid. Both baselines are near chance there.

2. **The baselines are wired correctly.** FatFormer scores 0.995 on DF-GAN and
   CNNDetection 0.966 — GAN families close to what they were trained on. A
   broken integration does not produce 0.995 on one column and 0.50 on another.
   What we are measuring is distribution shift, not a bug.

3. **The split is by generator era, not by difficulty.** Both baselines are
   ProGAN-supervised and read high-frequency GAN fingerprints. On 2024–2025
   generators (GPT-Image-2, Nano Banana, DALL·E-advanced) CNNDetection is at
   chance — 0.501, 0.511, 0.586 — because those fingerprints are gone. We are
   ahead there and behind on the old families, which is the trade we chose:
   CLIP semantics generalise forward, spectral fingerprints do not.

4. **Robustness is ours by a wide margin.** Our worst-case drop across the grid
   is 0.030 on the organizer set and ≤0.019 on the modern generators; FatFormer
   swings 0.394 and 0.347. Its jpeg30 column is not even monotone — 0.883 on the
   organizer set against 0.646 clean, 0.364 on GigaGAN against 0.711 clean.
   A detector whose ranking reverses under JPEG cannot be given a threshold.

5. **Where we lose outright: VQ-VAE FFHQ at 0.224**, well below chance, and ADM
   at 0.483. The VQ-VAE column is a *reconstruction* set — a real FFHQ face
   pushed through an autoencoder — so the general probe reads it as globally
   authentic, exactly the failure mode `docs/robustness.md` describes for local
   tampering (AUROC 0.37). It is the same hole, not a new one. ADM is
   256×256 diffusion output, the family both baselines were tuned against.

## Caveats

- The probe sets are 150 images each. Differences under ~0.05 AUROC there are
  noise; the organizer-set numbers (600 vs 600) are the solid ones.
- The real pool is shared across generators and is LAION, while the fakes come
  from different sources — VQ-VAE FFHQ is faces against a general real pool, so
  that column confounds "generator" with "content".
- Neither baseline was retrained or calibrated on our data, and we were not
  retrained on theirs. This compares released detectors as shipped.

## Reproduce

```bash
# weights (not in the repo, ~2.3GB)
curl -L "https://www.dropbox.com/s/2g2jagq2jn1fd0i/blur_jpg_prob0.5.pth?dl=1" -o w/blur_jpg_prob0.5.pth
python -c "import gdown; gdown.download(id='1Q_Kgq4ygDf8XEHgAf-SgDN6Ru_IOTLkj', output='w/fatformer_4class.pth')"
git clone https://github.com/Michel-liu/FatFormer && cd FatFormer && mkdir pretrained
curl -L "https://openaipublic.azureedge.net/clip/models/b8cca3fd41ae0c99ba7e8951adf17d267cdb84cd88be6f7c2e0eca1737a03836/ViT-L-14.pt" -o pretrained/ViT-L-14.pt
pip install pytorch_wavelets PyWavelets      # FatFormer's frequency branch

python scripts/compare_baselines.py --weights-dir w --fatformer-dir FatFormer
python scripts/compare_baselines.py --render   # tables from docs/baselines.json
```

~20 min on a 4060 laptop, CPU-bound on the degradations. The released FatFormer
checkpoint needs `--num_vit_adapter 3` (the README's eval command), **not** the
argument parser's default of 8, or the state dict will not load.
